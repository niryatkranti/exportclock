Timing

🕐 Golden Hours to Call

  • Middle East (UAE/Saudi/Oman): 10:30–13:00 IST or 15:00–17:30 IST (not Fri)
  • Europe (UK/Germany/Italy): 13:30–17:30 IST
  • USA East Coast: 18:30–22:00 IST
  • USA West Coast: 21:30–01:00 IST
  • Southeast Asia: 08:00–11:30 IST
  • Japan / Korea: 06:30–09:30 IST
  • Australia (Sydney): 05:00–08:30 IST
Banking

🏦 Wire Transfer Cutoffs

  • India SWIFT: Most banks stop by 15:00 IST
  • UAE (AED/USD): Initiate before 14:00 IST
  • Europe SEPA (EUR): Cutoff ~16:00 IST
  • USA Fedwire: Closes 01:30 IST next day
  • China CIPS (CNY): Cutoff ~14:30 IST
  • Always add 1 business day buffer
Holidays

📅 Blackout Periods

  • China Golden Week: Oct 1–7 — full shutdown
  • China New Year: Jan/Feb — 2–3 week shutdown
  • Middle East Ramadan: Shorter hours, slower decisions
  • Europe August: Aug 10–25 — buyers on leave
  • USA Thanksgiving: Late Nov — logistics slow
  • Japan Golden Week: Apr 29–May 5 — complete shutdown
Communication

💬 Email Timing

  • Target 8–9 AM their local time for best open rates
  • Europe buyers: Send at 07:00–08:00 IST
  • US East buyers: Send at 18:30–19:30 IST
  • Middle East: Send Sun–Thu, avoid Fri–Sat
  • ME/Africa: WhatsApp more effective than email
  • Always state IST when proposing call times
Logistics

🚢 Port & Shipping

  • Shipping lines cutoff: typically 48h before ETD
  • Customs operates Mon–Fri only in most countries
  • US ISF filing: 24h before vessel loading
  • EU Entry Summary: 24h before arrival
  • Avoid vessels arriving Friday — weekend demurrage risk
  • Avoid Chinese port departures Oct 1–7
Etiquette

🤝 Buyer Time Zone Tips

  • Always confirm buyer's local time, not just IST
  • Germany / Japan: Never be late — even 5 min matters
  • Middle East: 15–30 min delay is normal, not rude
  • USA: Keep calls under 30 min — they're time-pressed
  • Send meeting agenda 24h before any formal call
  • Avoid scheduling during their lunch hours
